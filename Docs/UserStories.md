AI Fitness App – User Stories

Epic 1: AI Fitness Assistant
 - As a user, I want to chat with the AI after my workouts so I can get simple advice about recovery and training.
 - As a gym user, I want the app to understand my workout intensity and give recommendations based on how hard I trained.
 - As a busy person, I want to upload a photo of my meal so the app can quickly estimate calories and nutrition for me.
 - As a beginner, I want the AI to explain fitness information in an easy way that I can understand without technical terms.
 - As an athlete, I want recovery suggestions based on my recent workouts so I can avoid overtraining and perform better.

Epic 2: Nutrition & Health Tracking
 - As a user, I want the app to track my daily calories and meals so I can manage my fitness goals more easily.
 - As a user, I want my workout and nutrition history saved in the app so I can look back at my progress anytime.
 - As a user, I want the AI to recognize food from pictures instead of typing everything manually.

Epic 3: Workout & Activity Tracking
 - As a user, I want the app to combine my workout data and daily activity so I can better understand my overall fitness.
 - As an athlete, I want to track training metrics like workout volume and effort level so I can train more effectively.
 - As a user, I want to see my workout progress and activity records in a simple dashboard so I can stay motivated.

Epic 4: Recovery & Recommendations
 - As a user, I want personalized recovery advice based on my workouts and daily activity so I can recover properly.
 - As an athlete, I want the app to show a Gym Readiness score so I know when my body is ready for intense training.
 - As a user, I want the AI to give short and helpful fitness suggestions that fit my daily routine.
