User Stories
    These stories focus on replacing the basic "SparkyAI" with your advanced, data-driven "ApexAI" coach and integrating your specialized athletic features.
Epic 1: The ApexAI Engine
    As a high-performance athlete, I want the AI to calculate my total volume-load and RPE from my natural language chat so that I get advanced athletic metrics instead of just generic workout logging.
    As a user, I want the AI coach to adjust its conversational tone based on my specific persona profile so that I get the right level of motivation and technical detail.
    As a busy professional, I want to upload a photo of my meal directly into the chat interface so that the AI automatically estimates and logs my macronutrients using external vision APIs.
Epic 2: Advanced Athletic Metrics
    As an athlete, I want the system to automatically combine the background step data (already captured by SparkyFitness) with my heavy lifting volume so that the backend can calculate a highly accurate, daily "Gym Readiness" score.
    As a competitive cricket player, I want to track sport-specific workloads (like overs bowled and match intensity) so that I can optimize my active recovery before weekend games.
